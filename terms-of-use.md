## 10. Content you import or send

The app lets you bring your own content to generate lessons:

- **MIDI and MusicXML files**, read and turned into a lesson **on the device itself**;
- **imported music** (in some versions): you paste the link to a page where the music is, or choose a recording or an audio or video file from the device. The app sends the link or the sound to Cascata's server, which gets the sound, separates the instruments and writes out the notes with the help of artificial intelligence. The finished lesson comes back **to you alone** and is kept on your device. From a video, the app sends only the sound.

We call all of this "Your Content", and the lesson generated from it an "Imported Lesson".

### 10.2 How imported music is processed and how long it stays

- Processing happens on **Cascata's servers**. The heaviest steps may be done by a **cloud computing provider that processes the audio on our behalf**, following our instructions, and that may not use it for any other purpose. Who that provider is and where it is located is in the Privacy Policy.
- The audio and the separated tracks are **deleted as soon as the lesson is ready** (or as soon as processing fails).
- The lesson files and the record of the request (the link or the file name, and the title) stay on the server **for up to 24 hours** after processing ends, so the app can fetch them even if you close it midway. After that, they are deleted.
- Once the lesson reaches your device, it stays only there. To delete it, delete the song in the app.
